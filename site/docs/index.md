## Peaky

Peaky is a local-first readiness assessment app. Template authors define what "ready" means as reusable templates — pillars and questions — teams run advisor-guided review sessions against them, and progress is tracked by pillar so gaps and trends are visible as reviews happen.

![Teams dashboard](assets/images/teams-page.png#only-light)
![Teams dashboard](assets/images/teams-page-dark.png#only-dark)

### Built for

- **Peak season readiness** — validate systems ahead of high-traffic events
- **Compliance-focused assessments** — SOC2, PCI-DSS, and similar control reviews
- **Product launch readiness** — confirm operational standards are met before general availability
- **Continuous readiness** — repeat assessments over time to track maturity and catch drift
