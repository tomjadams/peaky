# Assessor quickstart

You've been assigned a review. Here's everything you need to know in 2 minutes.

## The assessment view

Open your review and you'll find questions organized by ^^pillar^^ — categories like Reliability, Security, or Operations. Each question targets a specific aspect of readiness.

![Assessment](../assets/images/product-assessment.png#only-light)
![Assessment Dark](../assets/images/product-assessment-dark.png#only-dark)

## How to answer

For each question, pick one:

| Answer | When to use it |
|--------|---------------|
| **Yes** | The requirement is met |
| **No** | The requirement is not met — a gap to address |
| **N/A** | Doesn't apply to your context (you'll select a reason) |

!!! tip "It's okay to answer No"
    A "No" is not a failure — it's a signal. The entire point of an assessment is to find gaps *before* they become incidents. Honest answers are more valuable than green dashboards.

## Your answers save automatically

Every answer saves to your browser instantly. You won't lose work, even if you close the tab or go offline.

When you're ready to share progress, click **Save** and add a comment. This creates a ^^session snapshot^^ — a timestamped record in the review history that your team and advisors can see.

## Gaps are a good thing

"No" answers surface as gaps. Your team lead and advisors see them and help with:

- Prioritizing which gaps to fix before the target date
- Sharing how other teams solved similar problems
- Deciding which gaps are acceptable risks vs. must-fix

Use ^^pillar notes^^ to add context — explain what's partially done, what the plan is, or what you need help with.

## Quick reference

- **Navigate pillars** — tabs at the top of the assessment
- **See why a question matters** — click the info icon on any question
- **Open command palette** — **Cmd+K** (Mac) / **Ctrl+K** (Windows)
- **Try survey mode** — focused, one-question-at-a-time experience
